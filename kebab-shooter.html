Kebab

A dynamic and humorous 2D arcade game where you pilot a flying foodtruck and defend it against waves of unusual enemies. Earn points and Kebab Coins, upgrade your vehicle and survive a world full of culinary chaos.

Watch gameplay ↗

THE IDEA

Small game.
Big flavour.

Kebab Shooter combines arcade action, a cartoon kebab world and a distinctive homemade sense of humour. Pilot a flying foodtruck, dodge enemy fire, aim carefully and fight off wave after wave of enemies. Every level brings new threats, with challenging bosses waiting at the end.

Defeat enemies, earn points and collect Kebab Coins. Spend them in the workshop to unlock a twin launcher, a faster engine, increased fire rate, reinforced bodywork, an auto-shooter and special weapons including the kebab knife, triple jaw and garlic sauce beam.

Complete the campaign to unlock Endless Mode — an unlimited fight for the highest score. The game is being developed primarily for Android; the mobile build supports button controls, swipe controls or both together, as well as gamepad controllers. PC and Steam Deck versions are also planned.
