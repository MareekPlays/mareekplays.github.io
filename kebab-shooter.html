THE IDEA

Small game.
Big flavour.

Kebab Shooter is a colourful, independent 2D game created in Unity by one passionate gamer. It combines arcade action, a cartoon kebab world and a distinctive, homemade sense of humour. The player controls an unusual foodtruck, fights off successive threats, earns points to upgrade it and tries to survive dynamic levels full of culinary chaos.

The game will be released first for Android. PC and Steam Deck versions are also planned.
