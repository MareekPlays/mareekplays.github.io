28 SEP 2026 · KEBAB SHOOTER

Kebab Shooter is now available on Android.

The wait is over: Kebab Shooter has launched on Android and is now available to download from Google Play.

Kebab Shooter is a colourful 2D arcade shooter where you pilot a flying foodtruck and defend it against waves of unusual enemies. Dodge incoming fire, aim carefully and fight through a cartoon world full of culinary chaos — with new threats and challenging bosses waiting at the end of every level.

Defeat enemies, earn points and collect Kebab Coins. Spend your coins in the workshop to upgrade the foodtruck with a twin launcher, faster engine, increased fire rate, reinforced bodywork and an auto-shooter. You can also unlock special weapons such as the kebab knife, triple jaw and garlic sauce beam.

From campaign to Endless Mode.

Complete the campaign to unlock Endless Mode — an unlimited fight for the highest score. Connect to Google Play Games, earn achievements and compete in the global leaderboard.

On mobile, the game supports button controls, swipe controls or both together, as well as gamepad controllers. Are you ready to survive the next wave, defeat every boss and become the greatest kebab hero?
